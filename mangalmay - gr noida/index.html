MANGALMAY ADMISSION FAQS

Questions? Let’s find your next step.

Need guidance on MBA or PGDM admissions? Share your details and Mangalmay’s admissions team can help you understand the options and application process.

Ask an Admissions Counsellor →
Which management programs can I explore at Mangalmay?

Mangalmay’s official management pages list MBA and MBA++. Contact admissions to confirm whether a PGDM intake is available for your admission year.

What are the eligibility requirements for the MBA?

Mangalmay’s MBA information lists a bachelor’s degree from a recognized university with at least 50% marks. Final-year candidates may apply provisionally; confirm current selection requirements with admissions.

Can I visit the Mangalmay campus?

Yes. The campus is at Plot No. 8 & 9, Knowledge Park II, Greater Noida, Delhi NCR. Contact the admissions team before your visit to plan it.

Can I get scholarship guidance?

Merit-based scholarship support may be available under Mangalmay’s current criteria. Ask the admissions team about eligibility and awards for your application year.
