Ask a Counsellor
Which programmes can I explore at Thapar for 2027?

You can request guidance for MBA and B.Tech admissions. A counsellor can help you review programme options and the applicable admission requirements.

What is the total Thapar programme fee?

The estimated B.E./B.Tech fee ranges by branch, while the MBA fee is listed separately on this page. Confirm the latest official fee schedule with Thapar before applying.

Is hostel accommodation included in the programme fee?

Hostel charges are planned separately from programme fees. The listed hostel amount is an estimate; confirm availability and current charges with Thapar.

Is there a refundable security deposit?

A security deposit may apply as per institutional rules. Check the latest admission fee schedule for the amount and refund conditions.

What does the application process include?

The process includes reviewing programme eligibility, completing the applicable application and following the current admission instructions from Thapar.

Can I compare MBA and B.Tech before applying?

Yes. Request counselling to discuss programme choices, eligibility, fees and the next steps for the course you are interested in.

How do I request Thapar admission counselling?

Use the counselling form on this page and select your course interest. A counsellor can follow up with admission guidance.
