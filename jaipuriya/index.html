JAIPURIA MBA / PGDM FEES

Compare fees across Jaipuria campuses.

Published two-year PGDM programme fees for the 2027–29 batch. Jaipuria describes its PGDM as MBA-equivalent.

CampusYear 1Year 2Total programme fee
Lucknow₹7,70,000₹7,55,000₹15,25,000
Noida₹8,30,000₹8,20,000₹16,50,000
Jaipur₹7,50,000₹7,25,000₹14,75,000
Indore₹6,80,000₹6,70,000₹13,50,000

A refundable security deposit of ₹15,000 is payable with the first instalment, in addition to the programme fee. Hostel charges and scholarships are separate. Confirm the latest details with Jaipuria before applying.
