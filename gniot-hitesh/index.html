ABOUT THE PROGRAMME

About GNIOT PGDM & MBA Admission

Understand the programme, admission route and support available for management aspirants.

GNIOT Greater Noida is a well-known name for management education in Delhi NCR. GNIOT Institute of Management Studies offers PGDM as a two-year full-time management programme.

Students also search for GNIOT MBA admission because MBA is offered as a two-year full-time programme affiliated to Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University, Lucknow.

Campus Direct helps students understand both options while providing guidance related to eligibility, GDPI, documentation and admission planning.
