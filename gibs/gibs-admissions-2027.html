PGDM Fee Breakdown 2027

The total programme fee for IIEBM PGDM admission 2027 is Rs. 11,60,000. Students can plan the payment in the given schedule of Rs. 50,000 + Rs. 3,70,000 + Rs. 3,70,000 + Rs. 3,70,000.

Ask a Counsellor
Which programmes can I explore at GIBS for 2027?

You can seek guidance for both PGDM and MBA admission options at GIBS. A counsellor can help you compare the programme path that suits your goals.

What is the total programme fee mentioned on this page?

The listed total programme fee is Rs. 11,60,000. The page also outlines the registration amount and the three planned fee instalments.

Is hostel accommodation included in the programme fee?

No. Hostel accommodation is optional and is planned separately from the programme fee. You can discuss current accommodation options with a counsellor.

Is there a refundable security deposit?

Yes. This page lists a Rs. 20,000 refundable security deposit, subject to the institution's applicable rules and conditions.

Where will the MBA programme be conducted for 2027?

According to the campus update on this page, MBA admissions for 2027 are for the GIBS MindValley Campus.

Can I get help comparing PGDM and MBA before applying?

Yes. Campus Direct can guide you through programme structure, eligibility, fee planning and the admission process before you submit an application.

How do I request admission counselling?

Complete the counselling form at the top of this page with your contact details and programme preference. A counsellor can then contact you with the next steps.

Is an application-fee discount available?

The page lists coupon code GIBSIMC3242 for a Rs. 250 application-fee discount, valid until 31 March 2027. Please confirm availability with your counsellor before applying.
