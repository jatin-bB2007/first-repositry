Ask a Counsellor
Which programmes can I explore at Chitkara?

You can ask about B.Tech, BBA, B.Com, MBA and PhD programmes. Confirm current offerings and eligibility for your chosen intake with Chitkara University.

What are the programme fees?

Fees vary by programme and specialization. Review the guidance on this page and confirm the latest official fee schedule before applying.

Is hostel accommodation included in programme fees?

Hostel charges are separate and vary by room type and facilities. Ask about current room availability and charges.

Is there a refundable security deposit?

A security deposit may apply under university rules. Confirm the amount and refund conditions for your programme.

What does the application process include?

It may include checking eligibility, completing the application and following programme-specific instructions. A counsellor can help you understand the next steps.

How do I request Chitkara admission counselling?

Fill in the counselling form on this page and select your course interest. A counsellor can follow up with guidance.
