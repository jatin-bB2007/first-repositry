XLRI FAQs

Common questions from applicants who are comparing B-Schools and preparing for the selection cycle.

Which programmes does XLRI offer?

XLRI is best known for its PGDM-BM and PGDM-HRM programmes, each designed around a distinct management and leadership pathway.

Is XAT required for XLRI admission?

Yes, XAT is a key part of the XLRI selection process. Candidates should align their preparation and application timeline with the official cycle.

Can I get help with the application strategy?

Yes. Campus Direct can guide you on eligibility, XAT planning, fees and the sequence of steps before you apply.
