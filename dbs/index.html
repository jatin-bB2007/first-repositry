DBS Dehradun admissions are generally based on academic merit and entrance-based criteria depending on the course. Students should check the current eligibility and selection process before applying.

  • Location: Mi-122, Behind Pharma City, Selaqui, Dehradun
  • Email: admissions@doonbusinessschool.com
  • Phone: (844) 924-6693
  • For MBA: bachelor’s degree from a recognised university
  • For BBA/B.Com: 10+2 or equivalent qualification
  • Application fee: INR 750 for UG and INR 1,200 for PG programmes
  • Scholarships are available based on profile and diversity criteria

What courses does DBS Dehradun offer?

DBS Dehradun offers several programmes including MBA, BBA and B.Com, along with business-focused academic options for students seeking management careers.

What is the fee structure at DBS Dehradun?

For MBA, the tuition fee is roughly between INR 9.7 lakh and INR 16.6 lakh. For BBA/B.Com, it is approximately INR 6.6 lakh to INR 8.1 lakh, excluding hostel and other relevant charges.
