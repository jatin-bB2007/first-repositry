PGDM/MBA Fee Structure 2027

The total programme fee for ISMS PGDM / MBA admission 2027 is Rs. 11,60,000. Students can plan the payment in the given schedule of Rs. 50,000 + Rs. 3,70,000 + Rs. 3,70,000 + Rs. 3,70,000.

Additional Fee Details

Hostel Fee

Rs. 1,80,000 per annum. Hostel is optional and can be planned separately from the programme fee.

Security Deposit

Rs. 20,000 refundable security deposit, applicable as per institutional rules.

Examination Fee

Examination fee will be charged as per the applicable university or institution notification.

Ask a Counsellor
Which programmes can I explore at ISMS for 2027?

You can seek guidance for both PGDM and MBA admission options at ISMS Pune. A counsellor can help you compare the programme path that suits your goals.

What is the total ISMS programme fee?

The listed total programme fee is Rs. 11,60,000. The page also outlines the registration amount and the three planned fee instalments.

Is hostel accommodation included in the programme fee?

No. Hostel accommodation is optional and planned separately from the programme fee. You can discuss current accommodation options with a counsellor.

Is there a refundable security deposit?

Yes. This page lists a Rs. 20,000 refundable security deposit, subject to the institution's applicable rules and conditions.

What does the application process include?

The process includes Campus Direct registration, counsellor verification, programme guidance and support with the official ISMS application.

Can I compare PGDM and MBA before applying?

Yes. Campus Direct can guide you through programme structure, eligibility, fee planning, campus life and the admission process before you apply.

How do I request ISMS admission counselling?

Complete the counselling form at the top of this page with your contact details and programme preference. A counsellor can then contact you with the next steps.

Is an application-fee discount available?

The page lists coupon code ISMSPMC3242 for a Rs. 250 application-fee discount. Please confirm the current offer with your counsellor before applying.
