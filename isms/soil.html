◉ Global Immersion

Cross-cultural exposure through international study tours

Through academic partnerships with POLIMI Graduate School of Management in Italy and Shizenkan University in Japan, students study alongside international faculty, visit companies abroad, and build the mindset to navigate an interconnected business landscape.

◉ Design Thinking Bootcamps

Turning ambiguity into opportunity

The best solutions begin with understanding people. Through immersive Design Thinking bootcamps, students work on real-world challenges by engaging with users, uncovering hidden insights, and rapidly prototyping ideas, building the confidence to solve complex business and social problems.

◉ Social Innovation Program

Creating meaningful impact through community immersion

Working with a network of 32+ NGO partners, students engage with real community challenges, gain first-hand insights into grassroots realities, and co-create solutions that foster empathy, social responsibility, and purpose-driven leadership.

◉ Experiential Fridays

Building leaders through shared experiences

Every Friday, students step beyond the traditional classroom to learn through storytelling, theatre, mindfulness, art, and collaborative experiences. These immersive sessions encourage reflection, strengthen human connections, and help students become more self-aware, expressive, and adaptable leaders.

◉ Himalayan Retreat

Finding clarity in the quiet

Amid the serenity of the Himalayas, students step away from the pace of everyday life to slow down, reflect, and reconnect with themselves. Through mindfulness, Appreciative Inquiry, and guided reflection, they gain a deeper understanding of their values, purpose, and leadership journey.

◉ Experience Learning

Experience learning beyond the classroom

Apply learning through live industry projects, simulations, and cross-functional teamwork. SOIL’s experiential model equips you to tackle complex, real-world business challenges from day one.

Ask a Counsellor
Which programmes can I explore at SOIL for 2027?

You can seek guidance for SOIL’s two-year PGDM and other management programme options. A counsellor can help you compare the path that suits your goals.

What is the total SOIL programme fee?

The two-year PGDM programme fee for the 2027–29 batch is ₹17.20 lakh, excluding the refundable security deposit and application fee.

Is hostel accommodation included in the programme fee?

Hostel charges are separate from the programme fee. Twin-sharing accommodation is approximately ₹1.08 lakh per year; confirm the latest charges with SOIL.

Is there a refundable security deposit?

Yes. A refundable security deposit of ₹30,000 is payable separately from the programme fee.

What does the application process include?

The process generally includes completing the application form, submitting the required documents, meeting eligibility requirements and completing SOIL’s selection process.

Can I compare programmes before applying?

Yes. A counsellor can help you compare programme duration, eligibility, fee structure and the learning experience before you apply.

How do I request SOIL admission counselling?

Use the counselling form at the top of this page and a Campus Direct counsellor will contact you to discuss your admission requirements.
