PGDM/MBA Fee Structure 2027

The total programme fee for GIBS PGDM / MBA admission 2027 is Rs. 11,60,000. Students can plan the payment in the given schedule of Rs. 50,000 + Rs. 3,70,000 + Rs. 3,70,000 + Rs. 3,70,000.

Additional Fee Details

Hostel Fee

Rs. 1,80,000 per annum. Hostel is optional and can be planned separately from the programme fee.

Security Deposit

Rs. 20,000 refundable security deposit, applicable as per institutional rules.

Examination Fee

Examination fee will be charged as per the applicable university or institution notification.

Important Campus Update for 2027

From the 2027 academic year, the MBA programme will be shifted to the GIBS MindValley Campus, the 10-acre campus. Accordingly, all MBA admissions for 2027 will be for the MindValley Campus only.
The campus location for both MBA and PGDM programmes is GIBS MindValley Campus.
